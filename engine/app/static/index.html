What kind of fight?

Suggestions fill the DMG budget with real monsters. Send one to the Fight tab to see how your party does.

Which build is better?

The candidates replace the first party member of the same class.

Each candidate replaces the member of its class and the party is re-rated on the benchmark steps that decide its rating, with the same dice as the base. Expect several minutes per candidate.

Results show the change in party rating with a 95% interval. ≈ means the interval includes zero.

Find a better build

Improves one member of your party one change at a time (kit, spells, feats, ability scores, species) and keeps a change only when it is clearly better. This takes hours on a 4-core computer.

The search log and each round's results appear here.
